9. GST reports
10. Dashboard

## Billing Rule

GST bills create official invoice numbers, GST calculation, customer ledger entries, stock reduction, cash or credit entries, GST sales reporting, and auditor exports.

Normal bills create normal bill numbers, stock reduction, customer ledger entries, cash or credit entries, and internal profit calculation. Normal bills must not enter GST reports.
